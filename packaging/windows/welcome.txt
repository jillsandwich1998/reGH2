reGH2 - an unofficial native PC port of Guitar Hero II (Xbox 360), drawn with Vulkan.

THE GAME IS NOT INCLUDED. You need your own copy of Guitar Hero II for the Xbox 360: the disc image (.iso), the extracted files, or the folder with default.xex. After Setup, open reGH2, point it at that copy and press Install: the game's code is rebuilt from your files on this computer (a minute or two). Nothing of the game is downloaded or bundled.

What you need on this PC:
  - Windows 10 (1809) or 11, 64-bit, with a graphics card and a current driver that supports Vulkan.
  - About 1 GB of free disk space for the program and the build, plus your game files.

Everything that builds the game comes with this Setup: nothing is downloaded and no other program (no Visual Studio tools) has to be installed.

This program installs for your user only (no administrator rights). Your saves are kept in %LOCALAPPDATA%\gh2 and are asked about, never silently deleted, when you uninstall.

This is a pre-release; see the project page for what has been tested.
